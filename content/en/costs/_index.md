## How the totals are calculated

- Running totals count from the start date to today: monthly items by the months started, yearly items by the years paid. A yearly item is paid up front, so it is not spread across days.
- The reserved IPs are billed hourly and converted to a monthly fee at 730 hours a month.
- The tables convert everything to USD before adding it up. The rates come from the download server's actual annual charge: 100 EUR is 115.24 USD, 777 CNY, 3713.80 TWD or 900 HKD.

## How to help

The community does not accept donations for now and has no plans to form a foundation. To give money, [donate to OSUOSL](https://osuosl.org/donate/); the [Acknowledgments](/thanks/) page lists what it provides to the community.

The [contributing guide](/contributing/) covers submitting ebuilds, fixing bugs and improving documentation.
