## License

The gentoo-zh logo is adapted from the Gentoo logo and distributed under [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/).

Copyright and license of the Gentoo logo:

> The vector versions of the logo are copyright Gentoo Foundation and Lennart Andre Rolland, licensed under [CC BY-SA 2.5](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/).

> The classic Gentoo logo is copyright Gentoo Foundation, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The “gentoo” letter outlines in the wordmark come from the Gentoo logo, whose typeface was identified as Zurich Light Extra Condensed on the gentoo-pr list (Sebastian Pipping, [Re: [gentoo-pr] Gentoo logo font](https://www.mail-archive.com/gentoo-pr%40lists.gentoo.org/msg00166.html), 2011-04-26); the “-zh” letters are drawn to match. The files here contain the letter outlines only, no font file.

The 中 and 文 glyphs come from [中-seal.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E4%B8%AD-seal.svg) and [文-seal.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%E6%96%87-seal.svg) on Wikimedia Commons; both file pages state: “This SVG representation of an ancient script is in the public domain.”
