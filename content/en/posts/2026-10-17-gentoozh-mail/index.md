gentoozh.org now offers `@gentoozh.org` mailboxes to gentoo-zh developers. To get one, write to [zakk@gentoozh.org](mailto:zakk@gentoozh.org) or ask in the [Telegram group](https://t.me/gentoo_zh).

## Forum mail

The [Oregon State University Open Source Lab (OSUOSL)](https://osuosl.org/) provides SMTP for the [gentoo-zh forum](https://forum.gentoozh.org/). It carries the forum's mail only.

Some mail notification services may follow.
